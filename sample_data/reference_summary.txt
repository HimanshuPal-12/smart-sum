Technology is changing education around the world through interactive whiteboards and online learning platforms, although online courses require self-discipline and reliable internet access. Adaptive software based on artificial intelligence can personalize exercises for each student and early studies suggest it improves mathematics scores. Concerns about screen time have led some schools to introduce device-free days for young children. The digital divide remains a challenge because not every family can afford devices or fast internet, so several governments now provide subsidized equipment to low-income households. Teacher training is equally important, since smart classrooms help only when teachers can integrate them confidently into their lessons. Experts predict that blended learning, which combines classroom teaching with digital resources, will become the norm in the coming years. In conclusion, technology can enrich education when schools balance innovation with equity, health, and the human connection at the heart of teaching.
